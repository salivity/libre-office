Hyphenation and Line Breaks in LibreOffice Writer

LibreOffice Writer provides several built-in tools to control how text flows across lines and pages. By configuring automatic hyphenation, inserting manual breaks and formatting marks, and adjusting paragraph text flow settings, you can eliminate awkward spacing and maintain clean, professional typography throughout your documents.

1. Automatic Hyphenation

Automatic hyphenation allows Writer to insert hyphens dynamically at the ends of lines based on the active language dictionary.

  • For Specific Paragraphs or Styles: Right-click the text, select Paragraph (or edit the Paragraph Style), go to the Text Flow tab, and check Automatically under the Hyphenation section.
  • Control Parameters: Within the same menu, you can customize:
    • Characters at line end: The minimum number of characters required before a hyphen.
    • Characters at line begin: The minimum number of characters required after a hyphen on the next line.
    • Maximum consecutive hyphenated lines: The maximum number of consecutive lines that can end in a hyphen.

2. Manual and Special Hyphens

When automatic hyphenation is not ideal, you can use specialized hyphen characters to control word splitting manually.

  • Soft Hyphen (Optional Hyphen): A hidden hyphen that only appears if the word falls at the end of a line.
    • Shortcut: Ctrl + - (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + - (macOS).
    • Menu: Insert > Formatting Mark > Soft Hyphen.
  • Non-Breaking Hyphen: Prevents a hyphenated compound word (such as "e-commerce" or a phone number) from breaking across two lines.
    • Shortcut: Ctrl + Shift + - (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + Shift + - (macOS).
    • Menu: Insert > Formatting Mark > Non-Breaking Hyphen.

3. Manual Line Breaks and Spaces

You can force or prevent line breaks without starting a new paragraph.

  • Line Break (Shift Return): Forces the text onto a new line within the same paragraph, preserving paragraph formatting and spacing.
    • Shortcut: Shift + Enter.
    • Menu: Insert > More Breaks > Manual Line Break.
  • Non-Breaking Space: Keeps two words together on the same line (useful for units of measurement, names, or dates).
    • Shortcut: Ctrl + Shift + Space (Windows/Linux) or Cmd + Shift + Space (macOS).
    • Menu: Insert > Formatting Mark > Non-Breaking Space.

4. Paragraph Flow Options

Advanced line-break behavior and page-split controls can be adjusted to prevent isolated lines at the top or bottom of pages.

  • Open Paragraph or Paragraph Style settings and navigate to the Text Flow tab:
    • Orphan Control: Defines the minimum number of lines required at the bottom of a page before breaking a paragraph.
    • Widow Control: Defines the minimum number of lines required at the top of the next page when a paragraph is split.
    • Do Not Split Paragraph: Keeps the entire paragraph on a single page or column.
    • Keep with Next Paragraph: Ensures the current paragraph stays on the same page as the following paragraph (essential for headings).

5. Language and Dictionary Settings

Hyphenation rules depend on the document's language settings. To ensure accurate hyphenation:

  • Go to Tools > Options > Language Settings > Languages to set the default document language.
  • Manage hyphenation modules and custom rules under Tools > Options > Language Settings > Writing Aids.
