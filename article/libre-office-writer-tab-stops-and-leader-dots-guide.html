LibreOffice Writer Tab Stops and Leader Dots Guide

Tab stops and leader dots in LibreOffice Writer offer a precise way to align text horizontally without creating complex tables. By setting custom tab stops—such as left, right, centered, or decimal—and pairing them with leader dots (fill characters), you can effortlessly create clean layouts for documents like tables of contents, menus, resumes, and price lists.

Understanding Tab Stops in LibreOffice Writer

A tab stop is a predetermined horizontal position on a page where the cursor stops after pressing the Tab key. By default, LibreOffice Writer places standard tab stops every 0.5 inches (or 1.27 cm), but you can create custom tab stops to control alignment precisely:

  • Left: Aligns the beginning of the text to the tab stop.
  • Right: Aligns the end of the text to the tab stop, making it ideal for right-aligned dates or numbers.
  • Centered: Centers the text directly over the tab stop position.
  • Decimal: Aligns numbers at a decimal point (period or comma), ensuring numerical columns align correctly regardless of the number of digits.

Understanding Leader Dots (Fill Characters)

Leader dots are repeating characters—usually dots, dashes, or solid lines—that bridge the empty horizontal space created by a tab jump. They guide the reader's eye across the page from the text on the left to the corresponding information on the right.

Common uses include:

  • Connecting chapter titles to page numbers.
  • Linking menu items to prices.
  • Connecting form labels to signature lines (using underscore fill characters).

How to Configure Tab Stops and Leader Dots

To set precise tab stops with leader dots, use the Paragraph Properties dialog:

  1. Select the Text: Highlight the paragraphs where you want to apply the alignment, or place your cursor on a new line to start fresh.
  2. Open the Paragraph Settings: Go to the menu and select Format > Paragraph, or right-click the selected text and choose Paragraph > Paragraph.
  3. Navigate to the Tabs Tab: Click on the Tabs tab at the top of the dialog box.
  4. Define Position and Type:
    • In the Position field, enter the desired measurement (e.g., 6.00" or 15.00 cm).
    • Under Type, select the alignment type (e.g., Right or Decimal).
  5. Add Fill Character (Leader Dots):
    • Under the Fill Character section, choose the dot leader option (. . . .), dashes (- - - -), a solid line (_____), or specify a custom character.
  6. Apply the Setting: Click the New button to add the configured tab stop to the list, then click OK.

Using the Ruler for Quick Adjustments

You can also interact directly with the horizontal ruler at the top of the document:

  • Switch Tab Types: Click the tab selector button located at the far left of the horizontal ruler to cycle through Left, Center, Right, and Decimal stops.
  • Place on Ruler: Click directly onto the horizontal ruler at the desired measurement to place the selected tab stop type.
  • Adjust or Remove: Click and drag an existing tab stop marker along the ruler to adjust its position, or drag it downward off the ruler to delete it.
  • Note: Fill characters (leader dots) cannot be enabled directly via the ruler; you must double-click the tab marker on the ruler to open the Paragraph dialog and select the fill character.

Example: Creating a Price List Entry

  1. Set a standard left-aligned margin for the item name.
  2. Open Format > Paragraph > Tabs, enter 6.5" as the position, choose Right type, and select the dotted fill character.
  3. In the document, type the item name: Espresso.
  4. Press the Tab key (the leader dots will automatically fill the gap).
  5. Type the price: $3.50.
  6. Press Enter to continue the format on the next line.
