Line Numbering for Legal Documents in LibreOffice

LibreOffice Writer includes robust line numbering tools designed to meet the strict formatting standards required for legal briefs, court pleadings, and contracts. This guide details the built-in line numbering capabilities in LibreOffice Writer, explaining how to enable, customize, and control line numbers across paragraphs, pages, and specific sections of a legal document.

Global Line Numbering Controls

The primary control for line numbering is found under Tools > Line Numbering. Within this dialog, you can toggle numbering for the entire document and configure baseline settings:

  • Display Format: Choose numbering formats such as Arabic numerals (1, 2, 3), Roman numerals, or letters.
  • Intervals: Set numbers to appear on every single line (standard for court pleadings) or at specific intervals, such as every 5th or 10th line.
  • Position and Spacing: Position the numbers in the left margin, right margin, inner margin, or outer margin, and specify the exact distance between the numbers and the text body.

Page and Section Restart Options

Court rules frequently dictate how line counts reset across a filing:

  • Per-Page Numbering: Configure the line count to restart at 1 on every new page, which is standard for 28-line pleading paper.
  • Continuous Numbering: Keep numbers running sequentially from the beginning to the end of the document, which is useful for discovery documents, transcripts, and contracts.
  • Section-Based Restarts: Insert section breaks to reset numbering or disable it for specific parts of the filing, such as tables of contents, exhibits, or signature blocks.

Paragraph-Level Inclusion and Exclusion

Legal documents often require selective numbering. You can control which text elements are counted via paragraph properties:

  • Exclude Specific Elements: Exclude headings, block quotes, signature lines, or blank lines by opening the paragraph properties, navigating to the Line Numbering tab, and unchecking Include this paragraph in line numbering.
  • Count Blank Lines: Toggle whether empty lines created by carriage returns are assigned a number.
  • Restart at Paragraph: Force a line number count to restart at a specific number at any designated paragraph.

Styling and Visual Formatting

  • Character Style Integration: Line numbers are linked to the built-in Line Numbering character style. Modifying this style changes the font family, font size, color, and weight of all line numbers simultaneously without affecting body text.
  • Separators: Add custom text strings or characters (such as colons or vertical bars) as separators between the line number and the margin.

Alignment with Legal Pleading Paper

LibreOffice allows users to match line numbering to vertical gridlines or pre-printed legal ruled paper. By pairing line numbering intervals with exact line spacing (such as 24 pt or double spacing) via Format > Paragraph > Indents & Spacing, line numbers align precisely with court-mandated 28-line pleading templates.
