LibreOffice Writer Text Frame Colors and Gradients

LibreOffice Writer allows users to enhance documents by applying solid background colors and customized gradient fills to text frames. Through the Area formatting properties, you can choose from extensive color palettes, create custom RGB or Hex shades, or configure advanced two-color gradient styles such as linear, radial, and axial fills to suit any design requirement.

Accessing Text Frame Background Settings

To modify the background of a text frame in LibreOffice Writer:

  1. Insert a frame via Insert > Frame > Frame Interactively (or select an existing frame).
  2. Right-click the border of the frame and select Properties (or navigate to Format > Frame and Object > Properties).
  3. Switch to the Area tab in the dialog box that appears.

Applying Solid Background Colors

Under the Area tab, selecting the Color option allows you to set a single uniform background tone.

  • Standard Palettes: Choose from built-in palettes such as standard, material, html, or tonal.
  • Custom Colors: Use the Pick or Add buttons to define custom shades using Hex codes, RGB values, or CMYK models.
  • Recent Colors: Quickly reapply colors used previously in the current document session.

Applying Gradient Fills

Selecting the Gradient button in the Area tab unlocks two-color transitional fills. LibreOffice Writer offers several gradient types and detailed customization settings:

Gradient Types

  • Linear: Transitions color in a straight line from one side to another.
  • Axial: Transitions from the center outward along a straight line in two directions.
  • Radial: Creates a circular color transition expanding from the center.
  • Ellipsoid: Creates an oval-shaped transition expanding from the center.
  • Square: Generates a transition shaped like a four-sided polygon.
  • Rectangular: Generates a transition matching the rectangular proportions of the frame.

Customization Parameters

  • From/To Colors: Select the starting color and the ending color of the gradient.
  • Angle: Adjust the rotation angle for linear and axial gradients (from 0° to 359°).
  • Center Coordinates (X/Y): Shift the focal center of radial, ellipsoid, square, and rectangular gradients.
  • Border: Set a percentage to define how close to the frame edges the gradient transition begins.
  • Increments (Automatic or Custom): Control the smoothness of the color transition steps.

Adjusting Transparency

To soften colors or create layered effects, switch to the Transparency tab within the Frame Properties dialog:

  • No Transparency: Keeps the solid color or gradient fully opaque.
  • Transparency: Applies a uniform percentage of opacity across the entire fill.
  • Gradient Transparency: Applies a separate linear, radial, or axial transparency mask over the background fill.
