How to Convert Text to Table in LibreOffice Writer

Converting raw, delimited text into a structured table is a fast and efficient way to organize data in LibreOffice Writer without manually retyping information. Whether your data uses commas, tabs, semicolons, or custom symbols as separators, LibreOffice Writer includes a built-in conversion utility that parses the text and generates a fully customizable table in just a few steps.

Step 1: Prepare the Text

Ensure your raw data is formatted consistently. Each line in your document represents a table row, and each item within that line represents a column, separated by a specific delimiter.

Common delimiter examples:

  • Tabs: Product [Tab] Price [Tab] Quantity
  • Commas (CSV): Product, Price, Quantity
  • Semicolons: Product; Price; Quantity
  • Custom characters (e.g., pipes): Product | Price | Quantity

Make sure each row ends with a standard paragraph break (press Enter at the end of each line).

Step 2: Select the Text

Click and drag your mouse to highlight all the lines of text you want to convert into a table.

Step 3: Open the Convert Menu

  1. Go to the top menu bar and click Table.
  2. Hover over Convert.
  3. Select Text to Table... from the submenu.

Step 4: Choose the Delimiter and Options

A dialog box titled "Convert Text to Table" will appear. Configure the settings based on your data:

  • Separate text at: Choose the separator matching your raw text:
    • Select Tabs, Semicolons, or Commas for standard formats.
    • Select Other and type your specific delimiter into the box (for example, a vertical pipe | or a hyphen -).
  • Options (Optional):
    • Check Equal width for all columns to create uniform column sizing automatically.
    • Check Heading if your first row contains column headers.
    • Check Repeat heading if your table spans multiple pages and you want the header to appear at the top of each page.
  • AutoFormat (Optional): Click the AutoFormat... button to apply predefined color schemes, borders, and styles before creating the table.

Step 5: Generate the Table

Click OK. LibreOffice Writer will instantly convert the selected plain text into an editable, standard table layout.

Post-Conversion Adjustments

Once generated, you can format the table using standard tools:

  • Hover over column borders to click and drag them to custom widths.
  • Right-click inside the table and select Table Properties... to modify borders, background colors, alignment, and text wrapping.
