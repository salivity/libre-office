Buy LibreOffice Commercial Enterprise Support

Organizations seeking enterprise-grade reliability, Service Level Agreements (SLAs), and tailored software fixes for LibreOffice can obtain commercial support contracts through certified ecosystem partners. Because The Document Foundation (TDF) is a non-profit entity that provides community-driven assistance rather than direct enterprise services, organizations must engage with commercial vendors to secure dedicated technical support, Long-Term Support (LTS) software versions, and custom deployment options.

The LibreOffice Enterprise Ecosystem

The Document Foundation maintains a clear distinction between the community edition of LibreOffice and enterprise deployments. While the community edition is free to download and suitable for individual users, business environments requiring guaranteed uptime, security patches, and regulatory compliance should deploy "LibreOffice Enterprise" software provided by commercial partners.

Certified Support Providers

Commercial contracts are purchased directly through certified partner organizations listed in the LibreOffice Advisory Board and ecosystem. The primary global providers include:

  • Collabora Productivity: Offers Collabora Office (a hardened, enterprise-ready desktop version) and Collabora Online (cloud-based document editing), complete with long-term maintenance, security updates, and direct-to-developer support.
  • allotropia: Provides enterprise support, consulting, custom feature development, and long-term maintenance tailored to public administrations and large corporations.
  • CIB software GmbH: Offers support, integration services, and customized LibreOffice packages primarily for enterprise and government clients in European markets.

What Commercial Support Contracts Include

When negotiating a contract with a certified partner, organizations can select from various service levels that typically include:

  • Service Level Agreements (SLAs): Guaranteed response times ranging from 24/7 critical issue resolution to standard business-hour technical support.
  • Long-Term Support (LTS): Multi-year maintenance for specific software versions, ensuring business stability without frequent disruptive upgrades.
  • Priority Bug Fixing: Contract holders can request dedicated fixes for critical bugs and proprietary file format interoperability issues (e.g., complex Microsoft Office file formats), which are then backported to the customer's version and upstreamed to the core project.
  • Deployment and Migration Consulting: Assistance with mass rollout via Group Policy (GPO), configuration tuning, macro migration from VBA to LibreOffice Basic/Python, and user training.

Steps to Purchase a Contract

  1. Audit Internal Requirements: Determine the number of user seats, platforms (Windows, macOS, Linux, or Cloud), and whether support is needed for desktop applications, server-based rendering, or web-based editing.
  2. Select a Certified Partner: Review the certified partner list on the official LibreOffice ecosystem portal and choose a provider based on geographical location, language requirements, and deployment model.
  3. Request a Proposal: Contact the vendor's sales department to outline your requirements, desired SLA tiers, and deployment timeline.
  4. Procurement and Integration: Finalize the enterprise agreement, obtain access to the vendor’s custom software repositories and customer support portal, and initiate the deployment.
