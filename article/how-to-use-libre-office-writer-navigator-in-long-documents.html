How to Use LibreOffice Writer Navigator in Long Documents

The LibreOffice Writer Navigator panel is a powerful management tool designed to streamline navigation, organization, and editing in extensive documents. By providing an interactive, structured overview of all document elements—including headings, images, tables, bookmarks, and sections—the Navigator eliminates the need for endless scrolling. This guide explains the core features of the Navigator panel and how it enables seamless movement and content management across multi-page documents.

Accessing the Navigator Panel

You can open the Navigator at any time by pressing F5, selecting View > Navigator from the top menu, or clicking the compass icon on the right-hand Sidebar. Once open, it can remain docked to the side of your workspace or float freely across your screen.

Hierarchical Heading Navigation

The primary strength of the Navigator is its ability to display a structured tree view of your document’s heading hierarchy (Heading 1, Heading 2, Heading 3, etc.).

  • Instant Jumping: Double-clicking any heading in the list immediately jumps your cursor to that exact section in the text.
  • Outline Levels: You can filter the display to show only top-level headings or expand the view to see subheadings, providing a clean table-of-contents view.

Effortless Content Reordering

The Navigator allows you to restructure long documents without manual cutting and pasting:

  • Promoting and Demoting Headings: You can change the hierarchy level of a heading directly from the Navigator toolbar.
  • Moving Entire Chapters: Selecting a heading and clicking the Move Up or Move Down icons moves that entire section—including all text, subheadings, and attached media—to a new position in the document.

Quick Categorized Element Browsing

Long documents often contain dozens of embedded elements that are difficult to locate manually. The Navigator organizes all content types into expandable categories:

  • Tables: Jump directly to specific data tables.
  • Images and Graphics: Quickly review and locate figures or charts.
  • Bookmarks and Hyperlinks: Access specific custom reference points instantly.
  • Comments and Footnotes: Review reviewer notes and citations across hundreds of pages.
  • Sections and Indexes: Manage text blocks and generated tables of contents.

Setting Reminders and Navigation Types

The Navigator includes a dedicated navigation selector tool that changes how the "Previous" and "Next" buttons function:

  • Instead of scrolling page by page, you can configure the jump controls to move specifically between images, tables, headings, or spelling errors.
  • You can insert up to five temporary Reminders at specific cursor positions and cycle through them quickly while cross-referencing different sections.

Page Jumping and Document Tracking

At the top of the Navigator panel, a page counter allows you to type in a target page number and press Enter to jump there instantly. The panel also tracks your current location in real time, showing precisely which section or object currently holds your cursor focus.
