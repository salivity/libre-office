Who Manages and Oversees LibreOffice?

LibreOffice is managed and overseen by The Document Foundation (TDF), an independent, non-profit organization based in Germany. This article outlines the role of The Document Foundation, its governance framework, and how it coordinates global community and commercial efforts to maintain and develop the popular open-source office suite.

The Document Foundation was established in 2010 by former core contributors of the OpenOffice.org project. Operating as a legal charitable foundation (Stiftung) under German law, TDF was created to ensure that LibreOffice remains free from the control of any single corporate entity, guaranteeing the long-term independence and open availability of the software.

Governance within TDF is structured to maintain transparency and community representation:

  • Board of Directors: The executive body responsible for managing the foundation's operations, legal responsibilities, financial assets, and trademark protection.
  • Membership Committee: An elected body that manages foundation membership, approves new members from the contributor base, and oversees foundation elections.
  • Engineering Steering Committee (ESC): A technical group composed of key developers that oversees software architecture, code quality, release schedules, and overall technical strategy.
  • Advisory Board: A consultative body that provides strategic advice to the Board of Directors, comprising corporate sponsors, non-profits, and public-sector organizations.

Development of LibreOffice relies on a hybrid ecosystem supported by TDF. While the foundation maintains the technical infrastructure, manages release cycles, and safeguards the LibreOffice trademark, code contributions come from a combination of individual volunteers and commercial ecosystem partners (such as Collabora and allotropia). This decentralized approach allows TDF to direct the software's growth while preserving its status as free, open-source software distributed under the GNU Lesser General Public License (LGPLv3+).
